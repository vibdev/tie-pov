How to tie a tie, step by step, from your own point of view

An interactive 3D guide to tying a necktie. Each move is shown the way you see it looking down at your own chest, with a mirror view beside the instructions. Go one move at a time, replay a move, or play the whole knot.

Knots: Four-in-Hand (schoolboy knot), Oriental (simple knot), Kelvin, Nicky, Pratt (Shelby), Half Windsor, Windsor (Full Windsor, Double Windsor), St. Andrew, Plattsburgh, and a self-tie bow tie. Left-handed mode included.
